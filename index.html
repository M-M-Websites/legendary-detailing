Interior car detailing

Your vehicle’s cabin is where you spend your time, and it collects more than it shows: dust in the vents, crumbs between the seats, fingerprints on the screens, and grime on the surfaces you touch every day. Professional interior detailing goes further than a quick vacuum. Every part of the cabin gets careful attention, so it looks cleaner, feels fresher, and is more comfortable to drive.

The Interior Detail covers vacuuming, wiping down all surfaces, cleaning the door panels and windows, and trash removal, starting at $45. It’s a good fit for daily drivers, family vehicles, and cars getting ready to sell.

Book an interior detail

Exterior car detailing

Your vehicle’s exterior is the first thing people notice, and in Memphis it takes a beating from sun, rain, pollen and road grime. Regular professional exterior care keeps a vehicle looking sharp and helps maintain its appearance over time.

The Exterior Wash covers the wash, wheel and tire cleaning, tire shine, and streak-free windows, starting at $20. Want more? Add buffing, UV protection or rain repellent, or upgrade to a handwash, to help the finish stand up to Memphis weather.

Book an exterior wash

Complete auto detailing

When the whole vehicle needs attention, complete auto detailing brings the interior and exterior together in one appointment. Because Legendary Detailing comes to you, it’s one visit with no drop-off and no waiting room.

The Full Detail combines the Exterior Wash and Interior Detail with tire shine and windows inside and out, starting at $80. For the most complete service, the Full Detail Package adds buffing, UV protectant and rain repellent, starting at $120. SUVs and trucks are +$20 on both. It’s a great choice before a special occasion, after a road trip, or whenever your vehicle needs a fresh start.
