FAQ

Questions?
Answered.

Don't see your question here? Call (901) 483-8862 and we'll be happy to help.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill out the appointment request form on this page, call (901) 483-8862, or email xavierwade38@gmail.com. We'll reach out to confirm the details and find a time that works for you.

What detailing services do you offer?

We offer Exterior Detailing, Interior Detailing, Full Vehicle Detailing (interior and exterior), and Custom Detailing for anything more specific. If you're not sure which is right for your vehicle, just ask — we'll help you choose.

How much does a detail cost?

Pricing depends on your vehicle and the service you choose. Contact Legendary Detailing for a quote — the more you tell us about your vehicle, the more accurate it will be.

How long does a detailing appointment take?

Timing varies with the service and your vehicle's size and condition. Reach out and we'll give you an estimate when we discuss your appointment.

What information should I provide when requesting a quote?

Include your vehicle's year, make, and model, the service you're interested in, your preferred appointment date, and any details about its current condition or areas that need extra attention. Photos are always welcome when you email.
